# TIA — Trade Intelligence Agency
## Foundation and first terminal shell

**Parent identity:** 6868  
**Institution:** TIA — Trade Intelligence Agency  
**Existing product:** AI Trading Dashboard XPIX (preserved; not renamed or replaced)

### Purpose
TIA is the institutional intelligence and research layer above the existing XPIX product. The first implementation adds a dedicated navigation page between Radar and Workspace. It is an additive shell, not a rewrite of either page.

### Brand system
- Locked logo: use the exact owner-approved TIA monogram asset. Do not redraw, reinterpret, change proportions, or turn it into a “77” motif.
- Palette: black/charcoal foundation; champagne gold, brass and warm gold as dominant accents; amber/orange is restrained.
- Patterns: original variations of pixel/data grids, radial geometry, network nodes, circular systems and polygonal meshes. Do not reproduce reference artwork pixel-for-pixel.
- Motion: particles assemble/disperse, radial expansion, node pulses, grid illumination and restrained geometric transitions. Motion must not distort the approved final logo.

### Visual intelligence systems
1. **Radial Geometry:** central asset and measured cross-asset relationships; show correlation/lead-lag metric, sample and window.
2. **Network Connections:** nodes for assets/events/strategies/liquidity; edges require a defined and traceable relationship.
3. **Circular Data Systems:** rings for strength, sessions, risk and exposure; every ring declares units and source.
4. **Polygonal Structures:** geometric views of multi-factor confluence and liquidity; no implied signal from shape alone.
5. **Dynamic Grids:** heatmaps for liquidity, volume, correlation, volatility, sessions and strategy performance.

### Trading interface modules
- **Candlestick & Market Structure:** reuse existing chart engine; optional BOS/CHoCH, swings, FVG/IFVG, order blocks, sweeps, sessions and HTF levels.
- **Volume & Order Flow:** Volume, Delta, Footprint, DOM, Time & Sales. Separate executed trades from resting orders and derived estimates.
- **Heatmaps:** liquidity, volume, correlation, volatility, session activity and strategy performance.
- **Portfolio Analytics:** exposure, correlation clusters, expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, R-multiples, attribution and funded-account limits.

### Seven specialist functions
1. Intelligence Director — orchestration and synthesis.
2. Market Intelligence — macro, news, geopolitics, catalysts, sessions and cross-asset analysis.
3. Technical Intelligence — Price Action, SMC/ICT, Elliott, Gann and multi-timeframe structure.
4. Order Flow & Liquidity — Footprint, Delta, DOM, Time & Sales and liquidity evidence.
5. Strategy & Innovation Lab — research, strategy discovery and indicator development.
6. Quant Validation & Risk — backtests, out-of-sample validation, costs and portfolio risk.
7. Trade Review & Decision — challenge, ranking, trade plans and learning.

These are seven responsibilities, not necessarily seven isolated model instances.

### Data integrity rules
Every displayed metric must carry a source, timestamp, unit, coverage and quality status. Do not label mock or unavailable data as live. Forex broker DOM is not a consolidated global order book. Correlation is not causation. Hypotheses about news impact must be tested against observed events. No automatic live orders.
