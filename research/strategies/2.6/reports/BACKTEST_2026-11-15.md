# 2.6 provisional plan — backtest on Hyperliquid gold (2026-11-15)

**Verdict: the plan as drawn by the Workspace indicator LOSES money on every timeframe tested, and does worse than random entries with the same stop / targets.** Do not trade it.

## Setup
- Data: Hyperliquid `xyz:GOLD` perpetual candles (`candleSnapshot`, the API returns only the latest ~5000 per interval):
  15m 5015 bars (2026-09-24 → 11-15), 1h 5004 (2026-04-21 → 11-15), 4h 1968 (2025-12-22 → 11-15). Perpetual, not spot XAUUSD.
- Engine: `modules/two_six_chart.setups` (same as the Workspace `s26Calc`, parity-tested), swing 5, wait 80 bars.
- Plan: u = leg ÷ ratio; limit entry; stop = entry ∓ u; TP1 = 1R; TP2 = 2R; stop wins a same-candle tie.
  Scored without partial exits: TP2 +2R, stop −1R, TP1-then-stop −1R. Waiting / open / expired setups excluded.
- Cost: 0.05 % of price per round trip, in R. Control: 20 random entries per setup, same side and u.
- Script: `python3 research/strategies/2.6/backtest_plan.py gold_15m.json gold_1h.json gold_4h.json`

Price reaches the stop (≈ 77 % retracement of the leg) before TP1 (the swing extreme) about 2 times in 3.
So the problem is the entry / stop geometry, not only costs: a 38.5 % pullback entry is usually early and the pullback keeps going.

## Limits
- Events overlap (several setups at once) → t-stats are optimistic; still all strongly negative.
- Gap fills scored at the planned R. No slippage model beyond the flat cost.
- The creator's real stop / target / filter rules are unknown; this tests OUR provisional plan only.

## Next hypotheses (pre-registered, to test on the first 70 % and confirm once on the last 30 %)
1. Stop beyond the leg origin (the swing the leg started from) instead of 1u.
2. Entry deeper: 50 % / 61.8 % of the leg, and 2.6 as a TARGET projection instead of an entry.
3. Require the bigger timeframe to agree (the ★ score) and only H2 sweeps.
