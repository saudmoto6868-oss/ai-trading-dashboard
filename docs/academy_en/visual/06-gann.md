## The fan

```text
      2x1
     /
    / price
   / 1x1  <- bounce
  /
 100 ----> time
```

| Event | Visual reading |
|-------|----------------|
| Price above 1x1 from a low | Relative strength |
| A break of 1x1 downward | Usually a move to 1x2 |
| A rejection at 1x1 | A bounce setup |

**Discipline:** the pivot on the real high/low: no manual moving after drawing.
